Roofing guides

Free Class 4 impact-resistant shingle upgrade with your approved insurance claim

Hail wrecked your roof once. Class 4 shingles make the next storm much less likely to wreck it again, and they can lower your insurance premium too.

Class 4 is the highest impact-resistance rating a shingle can earn under the UL 2218 standard, which tests shingles by dropping 2-inch steel balls on them, roughly the punch of severe hail. Standard shingles crack and bruise. Class 4 shingles shrug off most of it.

Our offer: a free upgrade with your approved claim

If your insurance claim is approved for a roof replacement, we upgrade you to premium Class 4 impact-resistant shingles at no additional cost. Your insurer pays for the roof you had. We put on a tougher one: the highest impact rating a shingle can earn, to protect your roof in Hail Alley.

Why this matters for your premiums

After a major storm sweeps a Texas ZIP code, premiums across that area tend to rise. Many Texas insurers offer meaningful discounts on the wind and hail portion of your premium for homes with certified Class 4 roofs. Ask your agent what your carrier offers, and we will provide the documentation your insurer needs. Over the years, the discount plus the avoided damage is real relief.

The fine print, honestly

Class 4 does not mean hail-proof; catastrophic hail can damage any roof. Some insurers apply conditions to impact-resistant discounts, and a few adjust how they cover cosmetic damage on metal roofs, so ask your agent the right questions. We are happy to walk through it with you.
