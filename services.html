Repair or replace? An honest guide

A repair usually makes sense when…

  • Damage is limited to one area or a few shingles
  • The roof is under ~15 years old
  • Decking and underlayment are sound
  • There's no widespread hail bruising

A replacement usually makes sense when…

  • Hail damage is spread across multiple slopes
  • The roof is near the end of its lifespan
  • Repairs are becoming a yearly event
  • Insurance has approved storm damage coverage

Either way, the inspection is free and the recommendation is honest — repairs are great business for us too, so there's no thumb on the scale.
