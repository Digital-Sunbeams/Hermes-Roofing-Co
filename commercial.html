Commercial systems we install

Flat and low-slope buildings need different roofs than homes do. These are the systems we put on Central Texas businesses, installed to manufacturer spec.

TPO & single-ply membranes

High-performance TPO, PVC, and EPDM membranes for warehouses, offices, retail centers, and other low-slope roofs. Reflective white membranes resist UV, ponding water, and punctures, and help lower cooling costs in Texas heat.

Certified installers: Versico

Modified bitumen

Multi-layer torch-down and self-adhered systems built to handle temperature swings, foot traffic, and everyday wear on demanding low-slope roofs. A proven, watertight workhorse for older buildings and flat sections.

Commercial metal roofing

Standing seam and exposed-fastener panel systems for commercial and agricultural buildings. The longest-lived option we install, with excellent performance in hail, wind, and sun.

Coatings & restoration

When a full tear-off isn't necessary, restoration coatings can add years of watertight life to an aging membrane at a fraction of replacement cost. We'll tell you honestly which side of that line your roof is on.

Commercial roofing questions, answered

What types of commercial properties do you service?

Office buildings, retail centers, warehouses, industrial facilities, medical offices, and multi-tenant and mixed-use properties across Austin and Central Texas. Every building has its own structure, usage, and compliance needs, so we tailor the system and the schedule to yours.

Can you repair storm or hail damage on a commercial roof?

Yes. Texas storms cause membrane punctures, seam separation, and water intrusion that isn't always visible from the ground. We assess the damage, provide temporary protection if needed, document everything with photos, and coordinate with your insurance provider so repairs move quickly and your operations aren't disrupted more than necessary.

Do you offer preventive maintenance programs for commercial roofs?

Yes. Our maintenance programs include routine inspections, debris removal, sealant and flashing checks, and drainage evaluations, with a clear written report after each visit. Regular maintenance extends the life of a commercial roof, helps prevent emergency repairs, and makes budgeting predictable.

Are commercial roof repairs covered by insurance?

Storm and hail damage is often covered, depending on your policy. We provide the professional inspection, photo documentation, and reports your claim needs, and we meet your adjuster on site. Coverage varies by policy, so we'll give you an honest read on what to expect before any work begins.

What commercial roofing systems do you install?

TPO, PVC, and EPDM single-ply membranes, modified bitumen, roof coatings and restoration systems, and commercial metal roofing. Our installers are certified by Versico for TPO and membrane systems. We recommend the right system for your building's structure, usage, and budget, not the most expensive one.

How quickly can you respond to a commercial roof emergency?

We prioritize urgent commercial calls and can usually be on site within 24 to 48 hours, sooner when an active leak threatens your operations. Emergency service can include tarping, water diversion, and immediate repairs to stabilize the roof, followed by a plan for the permanent fix.

Do you provide commercial roof inspections and certifications?

Yes. We provide detailed inspections and certifications for property sales, insurance requirements, and compliance reviews, documented with photos and a written report you can hand to a buyer, insurer, or lender.
