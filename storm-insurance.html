After a storm passes: your checklist

  • Check gutters, downspouts, and window screens for dents from the ground
  • Look for shingle granules collecting at downspout exits
  • Photograph any hail on the ground next to a coin or tape measure
  • Note the date and time of the storm for your claim
  • Book a free drone inspection before signing anything with a door-knocker

Signs your roof took hail damage

Hail damage isn't always obvious from the ground. Look for dented gutters or downspouts, granules collecting at the bottom of downspouts, bruised or cracked shingles, and damage to soft metals like roof vents and flashing. If a storm has passed through your neighborhood, a free drone inspection is the safest way to know for sure, and photo documentation from a professional makes your insurance claim far stronger.
