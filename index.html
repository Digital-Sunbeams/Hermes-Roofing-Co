Common questions

How fast can you get to my roof after a storm?

We prioritize urgent storm damage and can usually provide an inspection and emergency service within 24 to 48 hours after a storm.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. We document the damage, meet your adjuster on site, and guide you through the claims process so your repairs or replacement are covered whenever possible.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

Our free inspection assesses your roof's condition and gives you an honest recommendation based on damage, age, and safety, with no pressure either way.

What roofing materials do you work with?

Composition and architectural shingles, standing seam and other metal roofs, wood shingle and shake, and commercial systems including TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen.

Is your work guaranteed?

Every repair is guaranteed leak-free for a minimum of 2 years. If a repaired area leaks during that period, we come back and fix it at no charge, normally within 48 hours.

Are you local to Austin?

Yes. We are family-owned, based in East Austin, and have served Central Texas since 2009. We are not a storm chaser.
